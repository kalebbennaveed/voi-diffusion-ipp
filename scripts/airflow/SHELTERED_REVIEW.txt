SHELTERED MONITORING-REGION REVISION (2026-11-01)

Why the old Scenario 2 upper route collided
The original right-to-left tunnel exhausted through the west boundary. Its
lower stream passed directly through monitoring region B. At the robot's
start (1.45,0.875)m, wind was (-2.582,-0.784)m/s, giving approximately
(-1.025,-0.311)m/s^2 air acceleration at rest. The unchanged wind-blind
tracker drifted into the west boundary at t=4.2636s, before the upper bypass.
Scenario 2 region averages were A=1.731m/s and B=2.166m/s.

Physical redesign
Same domain 8x4.625x2m, circular columns, region rectangles, and air properties.
The east central opening now lies at y=1.60..2.275m, below A; peak 5m/s,
westward direction. Scenario 2's second east opening lies at y=.35..1.15m;
peak 8m/s, direction (-1,-.6,0), normalized. Both openings use a compact sin^2
profile across y and a sine profile in z. The west boundary is closed.
The new pressure outlet is on the south perimeter at x=2.95..4.45m, outside B.
The rest of the perimeter, floor, and roof use no-slip conditions.
Blue and teal boundary segments on the page show these air openings.
Boundary openings, like all planar boundaries, use the existing finite grid.
No new solids, hidden windbreaks, or robot-obstacle replacements are added.

Each field is newly solved using the same 3D MRT LBM / Smagorinsky LES and
molecular viscosity. Interior wind is not masked, capped, or blended in the
monitoring regions. The stationary field is the vector mean over 16..32s of
a 32s calculation, sampled at z=.75m. It stays fixed throughout robot runs.

Computed 64x37x16 horizontal-slice measurements
Scenario 1: A mean .329m/s (95th .960); B mean .376m/s (95th .599).
Scenario 2: A mean .361m/s (95th .919); B mean .248m/s (95th .732).
Small faster patches remain near passage edges: Scenario 2 maxima are
A=3.438m/s, B=1.646m/s. Calm does not mean exactly zero everywhere.
Central passage mean speeds: 1.550m/s (S1), 2.136m/s (S2).
Lower passage mean speeds: .151m/s (S1), 3.501m/s (S2).
Upper passage mean speeds: .356m/s (S1), .613m/s (S2).
These regional/passages measurements use the masks recorded in the exporter.

Robot result
All 12 cases were recomputed. All 12 reference arrays compare exactly equal
to their previous versions, including timing. The robot, force parameters,
tracker, actuator bounds, backup, spline extrema, and QP are unchanged.
Scenario 2 unfiltered upper B->A reaches A, minimum clearance .09005m,
final goal error .00082m, maximum tracking error .2902m, maximum component
control .7649m/s^2. Upper A->B reaches B, clearance .14941m, final error
.00444m, maximum tracking error .02969m, control .8212m/s^2. Both also complete
with RPCBF. Halving the integration substep changes those upper-route
trajectories by no more than .0011mm, with identical outcomes.

Limits and unfavorable outcomes
The full-scene CFD remains provisional: grid convergence and experimental
accuracy are not established. This is a designed wind-tunnel scenario.
The difficult S2 lower B->A RPCBF run has 11 infeasible QP evaluations,
minimum physical clearance .0384m (below the requested .06m margin), and
about .124m path sensitivity under time-step refinement. These are displayed,
not hidden behind the eventual target-region arrival. It is not a safety or
impassability proof. The upper-route result is conditional on these fields.

Reproduce airflow from the downloadable bundle
Use Python 3.11 and install requirements.txt. Set PYSTENCILS_CONFIG to the
included compiler.json and PYSTENCILS_CACHE_DIR to a writable directory.
From the bundle root, for S=1 and S=2:
  python scripts/airflow/simulate_sheltered_airflow.py --nx 64 --scenario S --seconds 32 --sample .1 --output results/airflow/sheltered_v2_sS_n64
Then:
  python scripts/airflow/export_sheltered_wind.py --nx 64 --prefix sheltered_v2 --start 16 --end 32 --site scenario-site
The original displayed fixed fields are included under
results/airflow/pre_shelter_stationary solely for before/after measurements.
The first redesigned candidate (southwest central inlet) is archived in the
workspace under sheltered_s1_n64 / sheltered_s2_n64. It was superseded because
its central jet mainly went around the lower side of the third column.
The chosen version changes the central inlet direction to west, allowing
column deflection to supply the intended central passage.
