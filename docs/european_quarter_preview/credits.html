Canal Quarter · provenance

This is an original, procedurally assembled 3D neighborhood inspired by the architectural references supplied by the user. It is not a reconstruction of Amsterdam, a photogrammetry dataset, a wind simulation, or a planner result.

The physical material textures and daylight environment are CC0 assets from Poly Haven:

  • Red Brick — Rob Tuytel
  • Clay Roof Tiles 02 — Amal Kumar
  • Grey Roof Tiles — Rob Tuytel
  • Cobblestone Floor 13
  • Brick Pavement
  • Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy — Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest

Three.js and matching rendering modules use the MIT license. The water normal texture is from the Three.js r170 examples. The drone visual is retained from the existing urban-quad viewer and its original autonomous-drone-racing reference. The local licenses directory preserves the source license notices and material hashes.

All diagnostic plots intentionally await recorded simulation data. The wind overlay is a direction illustration. The travel-distance tool uses only the drawn route length and a user-selected constant ground speed; it does not run the planner or nonlinear vehicle model.
